This is my first outing with KICAD and PCB design; forgive stupidity and ignorance.
====

== Specs

|===
| Parameter | Original PB-3 | This Design
| Chemistry | Lead-acid | LiPo
| Cell voltage | 2V | 3.7V nominal
| Output voltage | 2V | 2.0V (adjustable 2.0-2.4V)
| Capacity | 470mAh | 500-1500mAh
| Charging | Proprietary | USB-C 5V
|===

== Safety

* Buck DC-DC steps 3.7V → 2.4V
* **OVP (Zener/TVS)** clamps output <3V if buck fails
* DW01A+FS8205A BMS: OV/UV/OC protection

== How This Book Is Built

This README is a step-by-step training example: it walks from raw
research to a working, physically- and electrically-compliant battery
pack, including the toolchain checks done along the way. Every step below
is its own `README.adoc` in its own directory, assembled here with
`include::` and `leveloffset` — nothing is a link, so the whole story
reads top to bottom in one document.

. *Research* — collect teardown photos, forum knowledge, and IC datasheets.
. *Measurements* — caliper the original pack and derive the mechanical envelope.
. *Design Notebook* — turn research and measurements into requirements.
. *KiCad Hello World* — prove the KiCad → KiBot → CI toolchain on a known-good project.
. *Mechanical Design* — OpenSCAD fit-check model of the envelope.
. *Circuit Design & Emulation* — KiCad schematic, component selection, ngspice model.
. *Architecture Diagrams* — PlantUML system diagrams.
. *Results & Compliance* — generated evidence that the design fits and works.

== Containerized builds

The GitHub Actions `Build` workflow runs OpenSCAD, PlantUML, KiCad, AsciiDoc,
and ngspice in containers. Generated board renders, circuit diagrams,
fabrication files, simulation logs, and documents are committed under
`results/`. The ngspice image and local runner are defined in
`containers/ngspice/`.
